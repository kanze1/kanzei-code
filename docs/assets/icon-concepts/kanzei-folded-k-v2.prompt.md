## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a final square desktop app icon for "kanzei", a personal AI coding agent built around persistent memory and continuous work.
Primary request: redesign the app's existing black-and-gold K identity into a distinctive, much simpler folded K emblem.
Subject: one bold, beautifully proportioned custom K monogram, constructed like a single broad ribbon folded at the center. A strong upright stem on the left and two clean diagonal arms on the right clearly read as K. Suggest a folded page and retained memory with only one subtle overlap facet. The silhouette should feel confident, compact, purposeful, recognizable and original, with precise angular construction and a few softly eased outer corners. Do not use an ordinary typeset letter. No triple stripes.
Style/medium: immaculate vector-like brand artwork, mostly flat solid fills; two restrained gold tones may distinguish the single fold. Design quality of a carefully custom-drawn contemporary desktop application symbol. NOT a physical object or presentation mockup.
Color palette: full-bleed uniform near-black #10130F background; the symbol predominantly muted warm golden brass #C9962E with a limited lighter warm gold #EAD18A fold face. Strong contrast, avoid orange-yellow or chrome.
Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas, one icon only, perfectly straight-on, symbol optically centered occupying about 64% of the canvas width and 66% height, generous even breathing room. Background extends to every edge, no second inset square or frame.
Constraints: preserve unmistakable K readability at a small taskbar size. Broad solid geometry and generous negative-space cuts. No thin lines, no fine details, no noise, no texture, no glow, no heavy gradient, no shadow, no bevel, no 3D rendering, no glass, no decorative nodes, no circuit traces, no AI sparkle, no brain, no robot, no infinity logo. No words, no labels, no additional letters, no watermarks. Deliver only the finished icon artwork, not a contact sheet.
